The **Cambrian explosion** is the interval early in the Cambrian when the animal fossil record suddenly becomes much richer and many major body-plan lineages become recognizable. The current international boundary for the start of the Cambrian is **538.8 ± 0.6 million years ago**.

> **Big idea:** It was not the moment animals appeared from nothing. It was a geologically rapid expansion of animal diversity, ecological complexity, hard parts, and fossil visibility after a long Precambrian history of microbes and earlier multicellular organisms.

Why does it look so dramatic? Probably because several things reinforced one another:

- predator–prey arms races favored shells, skeletons, burrowing, eyes, and mobility;
- developmental toolkits could generate more body forms;
- environmental oxygenation may have made larger, more active animals easier to sustain;
- mineralized hard parts and exceptional fossil deposits greatly improved preservation.

The word **explosion** is geological, not instantaneous. The diversification unfolded over **tens of millions of years**, and several animal groups have roots before the formal Cambrian boundary.
